4. Why E-Waste is Hazardous

Old screens and circuit boards contain lead and mercury. These are toxic and can damage the brain and nerves. They also pollute soil and water.

Batteries and power banks can catch fire or explode if crushed in the garbage. Never throw them in the dustbin.

Chargers, cables and old batteries contain cadmium. Burning cables releases poisonous smoke which harms our lungs.

  1. Take a backup of your data.
  2. Reset the phone or laptop.
  3. Remove SIM card and memory card.
  4. Keep all items together for pickup.
